FAQ

Questions? Answers.

How do I get access to Premium features?
Subscribe to Liquify Premium monthly or annually from inside the app. Both plans come with a one week free trial so you can try everything first.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Choose Liquify and tap Cancel Subscription. For more help, visit Apple Support.
Where is my data stored?
Everything stays on your device. There's no account to create, and if you turn on backups they're saved to your own private iCloud Drive.
Will there be an Android app?
There are no plans for Android right now. Liquify is made by a single developer, so rebuilding it for Android would be a big job. If that changes, it'll be announced here.
